# Tutorial Quality Standard (this course)

Every **Full-App** and **Build-This-App** guide must be usable **alone**, without the student guessing.

## Required sections

1. **What you are building** (one plain paragraph)  
2. **Time + minimum level**  
3. **Features checklist** (tick boxes)  
4. **Architecture / folders**  
5. **Step-by-step build order** (numbered; each step has a clear “done when”)  
6. **Key code** (enough to not invent structure from scratch)  
7. **How to test** (manual script)  
8. **Common mistakes**  
9. **Portfolio blurb**  

## Writing rules

- Short sentences. No jargon without a one-line definition.  
- Prefer tables and checklists over walls of text.  
- Never say “implement the rest yourself” for core features. Stretch goals only at the end.  
- One primary state approach per app (do not mix Provider + Riverpod + BLoC unless the lesson is comparison).  

## Depth bar

| Type | Minimum |
|------|---------|
| Full-App tutorial | Enough that a motivated beginner after the min level can finish without Googling structure |
| Build-This-App | Completable in the stated hours with acceptance checks |

If a guide is under ~100 lines and is a Full-App, it is **not done**.
