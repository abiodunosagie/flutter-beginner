## Teaching Style Guidelines

1. **Explain like teaching a 5-year-old**
   - Simple language
   - Real-world analogies
   - Visual ASCII diagrams

2. **Consistent Structure**
   - "Think of it Like This" sections
   - Visual diagrams in ASCII
   - Progressive examples
   - Hands-on exercises

3. **State Management Coverage**
   - Provider (beginner-friendly)
   - Riverpod (modern, recommended)
   - BLoC (enterprise patterns)
